The Yellow Couch started because I needed it. I needed a space where practitioners could talk about the real stuff — the burnout, the hard sessions, the legal proceedings nobody prepared us for, the calling that sometimes feels heavier than it does holy.

"I am not a seasoned expert looking back at the trenches from a comfortable distance. I am in them — with you. That is the only credential I need to host this conversation."

The Yellow Couch is inclusive by design. This is not a faith-only space — though faith will show up here because it shows up in my life. It is a space for every practitioner who carries the weight of someone else's pain and needs somewhere to set it down for a minute.

Grab a seat. You're welcome here.

What You'll Find Here

Not another clinical training.
Something better.

01
Real Talk About the Work

The cases that stayed with you. The sessions that fell apart. The moments you sat in the parking lot and couldn't go inside. We talk about those — because pretending they don't happen doesn't make them stop happening.

02
Clinician Wellbeing

Burnout, compassion fatigue, vicarious trauma, sustainability. The Yellow Couch takes clinician mental health seriously — not as a sidebar, but as the main conversation.

03
Professional Development

Supervision strategies, documentation, legal preparedness, ethical dilemmas — the practical knowledge that nobody handed you on graduation day and everybody expects you to have.

04
Guest Conversations

Clinicians, supervisors, counselor educators, and practitioners across specialties sharing what they wish someone had told them. Real people, real experience, real talk.

05
Faith & Meaning

For the practitioner whose work is rooted in something bigger. We talk about calling, doubt, grace, and what it means to do holy work on the hardest days — without requiring faith to walk through the door.

06
Community

The Yellow Couch is a community, not just a podcast. A space to connect with other practitioners who understand the weight — and who are figuring it out alongside you.
